A Room is an optional grouping — "Reptile Room", "Fish Room", a whole rack — that holds one or more Enclosures together. It's useful once you have more than a couple of Enclosures to manage, or when something (a space heater, a room thermometer) covers the whole group rather than one Enclosure specifically.

What you'll see

  • The Room's name, with its Enclosures listed underneath it.
  • Any Sensors or Plugs that belong to the Room itself, not to one specific Enclosure inside it.
  • A Light Schedule, if the Room controls lighting for the group as a whole.
  • A Fallback Sensor, if one of the Room's own Sensors stands in for an Enclosure that doesn't have its own.

What you can do

  • Create, rename, or delete a Room.
  • Add a Room-level Sensor or Plug — for example, one ambient temperature Sensor shared by every Enclosure on the same rack that doesn't have its own.
  • Set that shared Sensor as a Fallback, so an Enclosure without its own reading still shows one.
  • Set a Light Schedule for the whole Room.
  • Move an Enclosure into or out of the Room, either here or by dragging it on the Dashboard.
