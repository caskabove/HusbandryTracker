A Plug is a power outlet controlling something physical — a heat lamp, a light, a fogger, a filter — attached to an Enclosure or a Room. It can be Smart (a Gosund outlet that reports its own state back) or Manual (you flip it yourself and tell Husbandry Tracker what you did).

What you'll see

  • The Plug's current state — On or Off — and when it last changed.
  • Its Controller Kind (Heat, Light, Humidity, or Always On), which describes what it's actually controlling.
  • Whether it's Smart or Manual.
  • Its Plug Group, if it shares a multi-outlet power strip with other Plugs.
  • A Timer Warning if a Smart Plug's reported state doesn't match what was expected.

What you can do

  • Turn a Plug on or off by hand, from here or from the Dashboard.
  • Set its Controller Kind and target schedule.
  • Link a Smart Plug's account, or add a Manual Plug by hand.
  • Group several outlets on the same power strip together.
  • Pause a Plug without removing it, when a device is temporarily disconnected.
  • View a Smart Plug's Data Capture history (power draw over time).
