The Dashboard is the first thing you see when you open Husbandry Tracker. It's a live, read-mostly view meant to answer one question fast: is everything okay right now? You don't need to dig into Admin to check a temperature, see if today's feeding is logged, or turn a light on.

What you'll see

  • Every Room, with its Enclosures nested underneath (and any ungrouped Enclosures below that).
  • Each Enclosure's tracked Animals, and a Reading pill for every Sensor metric it tracks (Temperature, Humidity, pH, and more) — colored green, yellow, or red against that metric's own Target Range.
  • A small Sparkline trend chart under each Reading, showing the last week or month at a glance, with the accepted range shaded behind it.
  • An "as of" timestamp on every Reading, so you can tell a fresh value from a stale one.
  • Each Plug's current On/Off state for anything running in that Enclosure or Room.

What you can do

  • Click Refresh to force a live read from a Smart Sensor right now, instead of waiting for its next automatic check-in.
  • Log a Manual Sensor's reading directly from its pill, with no trip to Admin.
  • Log a Care Event — Feeding, Watering, Spot Clean, or Deep Clean — for an Enclosure in one click.
  • Check Plug on or off to insure proper operations.
  • Drag an Enclosure panel onto a different Room to reassign it.
  • Click a Sparkline to jump straight to that Sensor's full chart on the Graphs page.
