Husbandry Tracker requires logging in — every page, every visit, with no exception for this computer or your home network. The first account ever created is automatically an Admin; after that, an Admin decides who else gets in and what they can do.

What you'll see

  • A Log In screen if you're not already signed in — username and password, nothing else.
  • Your username and role shown in the top-right of every page, next to a Log Out button.
  • On Admin → Users (Admins only): every account, its role, whether it's active, and actions for each one.

Admin vs. User

  • Admin can do everything — manage devices and app settings, and manage every other account: create one, disable or re-enable it, change its role, or reset its password. There must always be at least one active Admin; the app won't let you disable or demote the last one.
  • User can use the Dashboard and log care the same way an Admin does, but can't reach device setup, app settings, or the Users list — those pages simply refuse to open for a User.

What you can do

  • Click Log Out (top-right of any page) to end your own session.
  • Add a new account: Admin → Users → "+ Add User," pick a username, password, and role.
  • Change someone's role, or disable their account: Admin → Users, using the dropdown or button next to their name.
  • Reset a User's forgotten password: Admin → Users → "Reset Password" next to their name — type a new one and hand it to them. This also signs them out of every device they were logged in on.
  • Recover a forgotten Admin password, with no other Admin around to reset it: right-click the system tray icon and choose Reset Admin Password. Pick the account from the active admins it lists, and it hands you a fresh one-time password on the spot — written down and shown once, never saved anywhere after that.

There's no email-based "forgot password" link anywhere in Husbandry Tracker — recovery always goes through a person (another Admin, or you at the tray icon), never an inbox.
